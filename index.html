Hansol Lee, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Scholar, University of California, Los Angeles

hslee226@g.ucla.edu

I study the neural basis of prosocial behavior in mice, and how social memory and familiarity shape who gives and who receives help.

Research

Current research
Social behaviors in rodent pairs and groups, social memory mechanisms, and the neurobiological basis of prosocial behavior.
Previous research
Memory allocation and memory recall, contextual fear memory, and hippocampal and amygdala function.
Technical expertise
In vivo calcium imaging, sleep recording, and machine learning‑based analysis of multi‑dimensional signals.
